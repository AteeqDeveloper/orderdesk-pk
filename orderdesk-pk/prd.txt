# OrderDesk PK — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

**OrderDesk PK**

### Tagline

**Manage Your WhatsApp & Instagram Orders in One Place**

### Product Type

SaaS (Software as a Service)

### Target Market

Small and medium-sized online businesses in Pakistan, especially:

* Instagram clothing sellers
* WhatsApp sellers
* Home-based businesses
* Cosmetics sellers
* Perfume businesses
* Shoe sellers
* Mobile accessories sellers

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many small online businesses in Pakistan manage their orders manually through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook messages, notebooks, and Excel sheets.

This creates several problems:

* Orders get lost in chats
* Customer information is difficult to find
* Delivery status is hard to track
* Payment records are not properly organized
* Inventory is difficult to manage
* Sales are difficult to calculate
* Customer order history is unavailable

Business owners need a simple and affordable system to manage their daily operations in one place.

---

# 3. Product Goal

The goal of **OrderDesk PK** is to provide Pakistani online sellers with a simple and affordable platform where they can:

* Manage orders
* Manage customers
* Manage products and inventory
* Track payments
* Track delivery status
* View sales reports

All from one centralized dashboard.

---

# 4. Target Users

## Primary User: Business Owner

A small online business owner who receives orders through WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook.

For example:

A clothing seller receiving 10–50 orders per day.

The business owner needs to manage:

* Orders
* Customers
* Products
* Inventory
* Payments

---

## Secondary User: Employee

Businesses may also allow employees to access the system with limited permissions.

Examples:

* Order Manager
* Sales Employee
* Delivery Manager

---

# 5. User Roles

## Admin / Business Owner

The Admin has full access to:

* Dashboard
* Products
* Customers
* Orders
* Inventory
* Reports
* Business Settings
* Employees
* Subscription

---

## Employee

Employees have limited access based on their assigned permissions.

Possible permissions:

* View Orders
* Create Orders
* Update Order Status
* View Customers

Employees cannot manage business settings or subscriptions.
